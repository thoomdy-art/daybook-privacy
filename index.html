DayBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

DayBook ("the app") is a trading calendar and journal. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Your trading data stays on your device

The trades, notes, goals and settings you enter are saved only on your phone. We do not have accounts, we do not run a server for your data, and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted. Backups and exports you create are saved where you choose.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) you are asked for consent first. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more: Google's advertising policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Fonts

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts when you are online, which means Google receives your IP address for that request.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not collect your name, email, location or contacts, and we do not use your trading data for advertising.

Children

DayBook is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page with a new date.
